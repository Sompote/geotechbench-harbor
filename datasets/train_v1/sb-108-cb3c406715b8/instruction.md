## Problem

A column delivers 300 kips to a square footing 3 ft deep, designed at a safety factor of 2.5. The profile stayed dry through the depth explored.

Site data give 35 degrees of friction at 125 pcf for the cohesionless sand.

Quantify the width required of the footing, b_req_ft (ft). Neglect depth and inclination factors.

Use Vesić's factors, N_q = e^(π tan φ)·tan²(45° + φ/2) and N_γ = 2(N_q + 1) tan φ, with the square-footing shape factors s_q = 1 + tan φ and s_γ = 0.6, and size the footing on the gross pressure: q_ult/FS = Q/B².

## Quantities to report

- `b_req_ft`: number in ft
