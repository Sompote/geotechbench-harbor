## Problem

The 1.9 m by 3.8 m footing rests 1.5 m below finished grade.

The sand tests 38 degrees in friction and 18.5 kN/m3 in weight, with c = 0. Groundwater levels off 3.5 m beneath the surface. The structure applies a uniform bearing pressure of 562.38 kPa; the required factor of safety is 3.

Evaluate the allowable bearing value q_all in kPa and a pass-or-fail verdict against the applied pressure. Neglect depth and inclination factors.

Use Vesić's bearing capacity factors, with N_γ = 2(N_q + 1) tan φ, and the shape factors s_q = 1 + (B/L) tan φ and s_γ = 1 − 0.4 B/L. With the water table below the base, use in the N_γ term γ = γ′ + (d/1.5B)·γ_w, where d is the depth of the water table below the base, and the full γ where d ≥ 1.5B.

## Quantities to report

- `q_all`: number in kPa
- `verdict`: verdict, exactly "adequate" or "not adequate"
