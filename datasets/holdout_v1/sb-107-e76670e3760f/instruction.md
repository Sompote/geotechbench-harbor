## Problem

A 3 m wide, 5.4 m long footing is founded at 1.25 m depth.

Strength runs c 5 kPa with phi 29 degrees; weight is 19 kN/m3. The profile stayed dry through the depth explored. The comparison runs concentric against 0.3 m off-centre, both under a 2.5 safety factor. LL 50, PI 24, moisture 32 percent round out the lab card.

Evaluate the allowable pressures q_all_concentric and q_all_eccentric in kPa, with q_all_governing marking the governing case. Neglect depth and inclination factors.

Use Vesić's bearing capacity factors with s_c = 1 + (B/L)(N_q/N_c), s_q = 1 + (B/L) tan φ and s_γ = 1 − 0.4 B/L. In the eccentric case B′ = B − 2e replaces B in the N_γ term only; the shape factors keep the full B/L.

## Quantities to report

- `q_all_concentric`: number in kPa
- `q_all_eccentric`: number in kPa
- `q_all_governing`: number in kPa
