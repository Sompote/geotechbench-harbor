# Geotechnical design problem SB-106-d2a697776185

You are a senior geotechnical engineer. Solve the design problem below. List the given data

## Problem

Plan: 2.6 m by 3.25 m; founding level 1.5 m down. Monitoring wells read 1 m to water.

Investigation logs a clean sand whose friction angle is 34 degrees at 19.5 kN/m3. Under service conditions the footing must carry 363.8 kPa at FS = 3.

Quantify the allowable bearing capacity q_all in kPa, and the verdict — whether the footing is adequate for the applied pressure. Neglect depth and inclination factors.

Use Vesić's bearing capacity factors, with N_γ = 2(N_q + 1) tan φ, and the shape factors s_q = 1 + (B/L) tan φ and s_γ = 1 − 0.4 B/L. With the water table below the base, use in the N_γ term γ = γ′ + (d/1.5B)·γ_w, where d is the depth of the water table below the base, and the full γ where d ≥ 1.5B.

## Quantities to report

- `q_all`: number in kPa
- `verdict`: verdict, exactly "adequate" or "not adequate"
